1. Website Privacy Policy

This section covers data practices for our website (performancebyz.shop).

Information We Collect

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • Server Logs: IP addresses, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps for security and analytics
  • Cookies: Small text files to enhance user experience and remember language preferences
  • Contact Information: Only when voluntarily submitted via email or contact forms

How We Use Information

  • To maintain website security and prevent fraud
  • To analyze website traffic and improve user experience
  • To respond to inquiries and communicate with clients
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. As a Swedish company, we comply with GDPR regulations.

Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may use trusted hosting providers and analytics services that adhere to strict data protection standards.

2. Mobile Applications Privacy Policy

Our Commitment to Privacy

Performance by Denise AB develops mobile applications with privacy-by-design principles.

No Personal Data Collection

Our mobile applications do not collect any personal data from users. We do not require:

  • Name, email, or contact information to use our apps
  • Device identifiers or unique user IDs
  • Location data or geographic information
  • Photos, contacts, or file access

No Tracking or Analytics

Our applications:

  • Do not use third-party analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Do not implement tracking technologies or advertising identifiers
  • Do not display behavioral advertising
  • Function entirely offline where possible

Data Storage

Any app data (preferences, settings) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties. Uninstalling the app removes all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Our applications do not target children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from any users, including children.

2. Mobilapplikationers Integritetspolicy

Vårt Åtagande för Integritet

Performance by Denise AB utvecklar mobilapplikationer med integritetsskydd som grundprincip.

Ingen Insamling av Personuppgifter

Våra mobilapplikationer samlar inte in några personuppgifter från användare. Vi kräver inte:

  • Namn, e-post eller kontaktinformation för att använda våra appar
  • Enhetsidentifierare eller unika användar-ID:n
  • Platsdata eller geografisk information
  • Foton, kontakter eller filåtkomst

Ingen Spårning eller Analys

Våra applikationer:

  • Använder inte tredjepartsanalystjänster (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, etc.)
  • Implementerar inte spårningstekniker eller reklamidentifierare
  • Visar inte beteendebaserad reklam
  • Fungerar helt offline där det är möjligt

Datalagring

All appdata (preferenser, inställningar) lagras endast lokalt på din enhet och överförs aldrig till våra servrar eller tredje part. Avinstallation av appen tar bort all associerad data.

Barns Integritet

Våra applikationer riktar sig inte till barn under 13 år, och vi samlar inte medvetet in data från några användare, inklusive barn.
